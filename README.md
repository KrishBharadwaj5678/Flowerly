# **Flowerly**

Experience the joy of planting flowers in your own environment with **Flowerly**!

---

### 🔧 **Installation**

📲 Download the app by clicking on the provided link.

🎮 Open the app and start planting vibrant flowers in your space.

---

### 🌿 **Features**

- **Plant Anywhere**: Tap on the ground to add realistic flower models to your surroundings.
- **Interactive Experience**: Seamlessly place, view, and enjoy flowers as they come to life in your environment.
- **Reset with Ease**: Clear all planted flowers with reset button for a fresh start.

---

### 🖌️ **How to Use**

1. Open **Flowerly** and allow camera permissions for AR functionality.
2. Scan your environment to detect surfaces.
3. Tap on a detected surface to plant a flower.
4. Use the reset button to clear all flowers and start again.
